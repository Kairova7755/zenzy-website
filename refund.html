Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

1. Digital Products and Services

Zenzy provides digital AI video services and digital Ruby/A-Coin credits. There is no physical shipping. Digital credits and Premium benefits are intended to be delivered electronically after successful payment verification.

2. Ruby / A-Coin Purchases

Ruby/A-Coin purchases are one-time digital credit purchases. Because credits can be delivered to an account immediately, a refund is not automatically guaranteed after successful delivery or use. Refund requests are reviewed based on the circumstances of the transaction.

3. Eligible Payment Issues

  • Payment was successfully deducted but the purchased Ruby/A-Coin credits were not credited due to a technical or provisioning issue.
  • A duplicate charge occurred for the same purchase.
  • A Premium subscription was successfully charged but the purchased subscription was not correctly activated due to a technical issue.

4. Premium Cancellation

Users may cancel an active Monthly or Yearly Premium subscription using the cancellation controls available in Zenzy or by contacting support before the next renewal. Cancellation prevents the applicable future renewal; access already provided for the current billing period may continue according to the subscription terms.

5. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, it is normally initiated to the original payment method used for the transaction, subject to the payment provider's capabilities and processing timelines. Bank or payment-provider processing time may vary.

6. How to Request Help

For a payment issue, email aikairova1@gmail.com and include your Zenzy account details, payment date, amount and transaction/reference ID. This information helps us investigate the transaction.
