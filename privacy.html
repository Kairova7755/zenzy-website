Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

1. Overview

Zenzy AI Video Studio ("Zenzy", "we", "us" or "our") provides digital AI video creation services through its application and website. This Privacy Policy explains the categories of information that may be processed when you use Zenzy.

2. Information We May Process

  • Account information such as name, email address, mobile number and profile information.
  • Prompts, images and other content that you choose to submit for AI video creation.
  • Video-generation requests, usage information and service status needed to operate the application.
  • Transaction and subscription information needed to verify payments, credit purchases and Premium subscriptions.
  • Support communications and information you provide when requesting assistance.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To provide and operate Zenzy's AI video creation services.
  • To authenticate accounts and maintain account security.
  • To process and verify digital credit purchases and Premium subscriptions.
  • To maintain Ruby/A-Coin balances and subscription status.
  • To provide customer support, troubleshoot issues and prevent misuse.
  • To comply with applicable legal, security and payment requirements.

4. Payments

Zenzy uses payment service providers such as Razorpay for payment processing. Payment credentials such as full card details are handled by the relevant payment provider rather than intentionally stored by Zenzy's application servers.

5. Uploaded Content

Images, prompts and other media may be processed by Zenzy or its service providers to perform the requested AI video generation. Retention can depend on the operational requirements of the service; do not submit content you do not have the right to use.

6. Security

Zenzy uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect account and transaction-related information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

You can contact Zenzy regarding account, privacy or support questions. Where applicable, requests will be handled subject to legal and operational requirements.
