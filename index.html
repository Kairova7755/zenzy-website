About Zenzy

Zenzy is an AI video creation application designed to make AI-powered video creation accessible through a simple mobile experience.

Users can create AI video requests through the Zenzy application. Depending on the selected generation settings, A-Coins may be required for video creation.

How Zenzy Works

1. Choose

Select Text to Video or Image to Video and configure your desired video settings.

2. Create

Submit your video request through the Zenzy application.

3. Generate

Your request is processed using the selected AI video generation options.
