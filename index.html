Refund & Cancellation Policy

Ruby Shop: Ruby/A-Coin packs are digital products delivered to the user's Zenzy account after successful payment. Because digital credits may be delivered immediately, refund requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

Premium: Users may cancel an active subscription using the cancellation controls available in Zenzy. Cancellation stops the applicable future renewal; access already provided for the current billing cycle may remain available according to the subscription terms shown at purchase.

If a successful payment does not correctly provision the purchased digital product or subscription, contact Zenzy Support with payment details so the issue can be investigated and, where appropriate, corrected or refunded.

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method where supported. Processing time may depend on the payment provider and banking network.
