Payment Use Case

What customers pay for

Zenzy collects payments for its digital AI video creation services. Customers can purchase one-time Ruby/A-Coin credit packs or recurring Premium subscriptions. Payments are used to provision the purchased digital credits or subscription benefits inside the Zenzy application.
